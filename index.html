Dr. Theophilus Kwofie

PK/PD Modeling Scientist | Applied Mathematician

Mathematical models and data analysis to support drug development and health research.

Current Position: PK/PD Modeling Scientist, Insight Global contractor supporting Johnson & Johnson Innovative Medicine.

Ph.D.: Applied Mathematics, Arizona State University.

My current work focuses on translational PK/PD modeling, including target-mediated drug disposition, model evaluation, and parameter identifiability. My research also spans mathematical epidemiology, population health, and social behavior dynamics.

Research Focus

I use mechanistic models and data to study drug behavior, disease dynamics, and the effects of interventions on health outcomes.

Current Themes

  • Translational PK/PD modeling using nonclinical and clinical data.
  • Target-mediated drug disposition and nonlinear pharmacokinetics.
  • Parameter estimation, identifiability, and model evaluation.
  • Mathematical epidemiology and public health interventions.
  • Modeling social and behavioral dynamics.
